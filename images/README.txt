=============================================================================
VERGIN CLINIC - IMAGES DIRECTORY
=============================================================================
Upload your real clinic photographs to this folder using these exact filenames:

1. doctor-portrait.jpg    -> Lead doctor portrait (3:4 or vertical portrait)
2. clinic-reception.jpg   -> Reception and patient waiting lounge (16:9 or 4:3)
3. examination-room.jpg   -> Sanitized clinical examination and diagnostic room
4. consultation-care.jpg  -> Doctor-patient consultation desk
5. service-general.jpg    -> General medical consultation and outpatient care
6. service-urgent.jpg     -> 24/7 urgent medical attention and triage
7. service-chronic.jpg    -> Chronic disease management and vitals monitoring
8. service-diagnostic.jpg -> Diagnostic testing and laboratory review

NOTE:
The website already includes verified high-resolution fallback photographs in
script.js and index.html so the website immediately displays real medical
photographs. When you add your own images here with the filenames above,
simply update the URLs in script.js under CLINIC_CONFIG.images to point to:
'images/doctor-portrait.jpg', 'images/clinic-reception.jpg', etc.
